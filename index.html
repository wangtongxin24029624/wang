A SMALL PLACE. A SIMPLE IDEA.

Your day deserves

Copper Cup is a fictional café designed around the small breaks that make a busy day better. Think coffee before class, a catch-up after work, or a quiet corner with a good book.

Our concept keeps things simple: a short menu, welcoming spaces and familiar flavours worth coming back to.
